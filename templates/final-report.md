# Review report

## Decision and rationale

State the artifact-specific recommendation (plan: proceed/revise/validate assumptions; implementation: integrate/fix/verify; PR: ready/request changes/insufficient evidence; document: share/revise/fact-check; UI: accept/revise/validate states; release: go/no-go/conditional). Separate successful review execution from artifact readiness. Explain the few reasons that change the next action.

## Scope and coverage

- Target and digest:
- Lifecycle stage:
- Intensity:
- Domains and methods selected:
- Reviewers, configured/runtime model provenance, and working languages:
- Budgets and enforcement:
- Checks actually performed:

## Accepted findings

For each finding: severity, confidence, location, failure scenario, evidence, impact, recommendation, source task, and verification decision.

## Rejected or unresolved findings

Record the claim, source task, evidence considered, and rationale for rejection or `needs_verification`.

## Coverage gaps and limitations

List failed, timed-out, blocked, skipped, or malformed reviewer tasks; unperformed methods; unavailable domains; model-diversity limits; environment assumptions; and evidence limitations.

## Next actions

Give ordered, stage-appropriate actions. For each: blocker or follow-up, responsible role, concrete deliverable and verification condition. Explain dependencies when needed. Further review must resolve a named uncertainty and have a stopping condition. Review does not itself authorize implementation, merge, publication or deployment.

## User-facing handoff

Deliver a self-contained summary in the project's working language: decision, important findings and consequences, ordered next actions, what passed and what remains uncertain. Link this report for detail; do not substitute the link for an explanation. State retained checkout paths and follow-up purpose where applicable.
